## Regulatory & Compliance Frameworks Cited

### Mandatory Compliance
- **SR 11-7:** Federal Reserve guidance on model risk management
- **GLBA (Gramm-Leach-Bliley Act):** Financial data privacy
- **CFPB Regulations:** Consumer protection and fair lending
- **PCI DSS:** Payment card data security

### Emerging/Upcoming
- **EU AI Act:** Compliance by August 2, 2026 (high-risk systems)
- **NYDFS Part 500:** New York Department of Financial Services cybersecurity requirements
- **DORA:** Digital Operational Resilience Act (EU)
- **GDPR/CCPA:** Data privacy requirements

### Call-Specific
- **TCPA (Telephone Consumer Protection Act):** Compliance for outbound calling
- **Bank Secrecy Act / AML:** Money laundering prevention
- **Call Recording & Consent:** State-specific requirements

### Platform Requirements
- SOC 2 Type II certification (minimum standard)
- Banking-specific LLM training (vs. general-purpose AI)
- No hallucinations/prompt injection guarantees
- Real-time audit trails with logging
- Data isolation and encryption
